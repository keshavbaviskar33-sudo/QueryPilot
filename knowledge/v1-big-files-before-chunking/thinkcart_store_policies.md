# ThinkCart Store Policies

_Last updated: 1 October 2026. ThinkCart is an online store selling electronics accessories, home goods and apparel across India._

## 1. Shipping

- Standard delivery: **3–5 business days** for metro cities, **5–8 business days** for other locations.
- Express delivery (metro cities only): **1–2 business days**, costs ₹99.
- **Free standard shipping** on orders of ₹499 or more. Below ₹499, shipping costs ₹49.
- Courier partners: BlueDart, Delhivery, Ekart.
- Orders placed before 2 PM on a business day are dispatched the same day.
- Tracking link is sent by SMS and email once the order is shipped.
- We currently do **not** ship outside India.

### Delayed orders
If an order has not arrived 2 days after the promised date, the customer may ask for a status check. If it is more than **5 days late**, the customer is offered either a full refund or a ₹100 store credit plus continued delivery.

## 2. Cancellations

- Orders can be cancelled free of charge **before they are shipped** (status: Placed or Packed).
- Once shipped, an order cannot be cancelled — the customer must refuse delivery or return it after delivery.
- Refund for a cancelled prepaid order goes to the original payment method in **3–5 business days**.

## 3. Returns

- Return window: **7 days from the delivery date**.
- Item must be unused, with original tags and packaging.
- **Non-returnable items**: innerwear, perishables, gift cards, personal care items with a broken seal, and items marked "Final Sale".
- Damaged or wrong item received: report within **48 hours of delivery** with a photo. These are always accepted (if reported in time) and the customer is not charged for return pickup.
- Free return pickup for orders above ₹499. Otherwise a ₹49 pickup fee is deducted from the refund.

## 4. Refunds

- Refunds are issued **after the returned item passes quality check** (usually within 2 days of pickup).
- Prepaid orders (UPI, card, net banking): refund to the original payment method in **5–7 business days**.
- Cash on Delivery orders: refund to the customer's bank account (UPI ID or account details collected securely via the refund link) or as ThinkCart store credit.
- Store credit is issued **instantly** and never expires.

## 5. Exchanges

- Size/colour exchange available for apparel only, within the 7-day return window, subject to stock.
- No exchanges for electronics — return and re-order instead.

## 6. Payments

- Accepted: UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, Cash on Delivery (COD up to ₹10,000).
- ThinkCart will **never** ask for a card number, CVV, OTP, or UPI PIN over chat, phone or email.

## 7. Warranty

- Electronics carry the manufacturer's warranty (usually 6–12 months, see product page).
- Warranty claims are handled by the brand's service centre; ThinkCart support can share the service centre contact.

## 8. Contact & escalation

- Chat support: 9 AM – 9 PM, all days.
- Email: support@thinkcart.example
- Complaints not resolved in 48 hours are escalated to a senior support executive.
