# ThinkCart policy: Shipping and delivery time
- Standard delivery: 3-5 business days for metro cities, 5-8 business days for other (non-metro) cities and towns.
- Express delivery: metro cities only, 1-2 business days, costs ₹99.
- Free standard shipping on orders of ₹499 or more. Below ₹499, shipping costs ₹49.
- Orders placed before 2 PM on a business day are dispatched the same day.
- Courier partners: BlueDart, Delhivery, Ekart. Tracking link is sent by SMS and email once shipped.
