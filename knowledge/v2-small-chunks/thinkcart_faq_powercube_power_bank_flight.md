# ThinkCart FAQ: Can I carry the PowerCube power bank on a flight / airplane?
Product: PowerCube 10000mAh Power Bank (SKU EL-103), price ₹1,299, 6 months warranty.
Answer: Yes. The PowerCube power bank is allowed on flights. 10000mAh = 37Wh, which is well below the 100Wh airline limit. Keep it in cabin (hand) baggage, not in checked-in luggage.
