# ThinkCart policy: Refunds and refund timeline
- Refunds are issued after the returned item passes quality check (usually within 2 days of pickup).
- Prepaid orders (UPI, card, net banking): refund to original payment method in 5-7 business days.
- Cash on Delivery (COD) orders: refund to bank account / UPI via a secure refund link, or as ThinkCart store credit.
- Store credit is issued instantly and never expires.
